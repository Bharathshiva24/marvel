Privacy

Last updated 27 November 2026

The short version

The Timeline is an unofficial fan guide to the Marvel Cinematic Universe. You can use all of it without an account. Signing in with Google is optional and only exists so My list and your watched titles can follow you between devices.

Without signing in

Your watchlist, watched titles, language and settings are stored only in your browser (local storage). They never leave your device.

If you sign in with Google

  • We receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google, plus a Google account ID. We don't ask for any other Google data or permissions.
  • We store your watchlist and the titles you've marked as watched, linked to your account, so they sync across devices.
  • A sign-in cookie keeps you signed in for up to 60 days. Signing out removes it.
  • Your name and picture are only shown back to you in the site's header.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your data, show ads, or use it for anything other than syncing your lists. There are no analytics or tracking cookies.

Third parties

The site is hosted on Vercel, and synced lists are stored in Vercel Blob storage. Sign-in is provided by Google. Trailers are embedded from YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com) and only load when you press play.

Deleting your data

Sign out to stop syncing. To have your synced lists deleted completely, email bharathshiva009@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and they'll be removed.
